A timeline that survives a phone

Twenty-four hours across 340 pt of phone width gives each hour 14 points, so a ten-minute session renders 2.4 points wide. No font fixes that. Below: the same real data, drawn both ways, at three widths — now with every block tappable, contended blocks showing both apps in both colours, renameable devices, and the theme, Mode and Range controls the original app already has.

Decision 01

Spend the axis you have in surplus

Scrolling down is free and native on a phone; horizontal width is fixed and scarce. So time — which needs unbounded room — goes on the free axis, and the app name goes on the scarce one, where text reads anyway.

At 64 px/hour a ten-minute session is 11 × 250 instead of 340 × 2.4. Room for a name, a duration, and the shading.

Decision 02 · revised

A contended block shows both apps, not a winner and a footnote

The first draft painted the block in the winner's colour and mentioned the loser in text. That reads as though one device was idle. Now the block is split into a band per device, each in its own app colour, widest band = the one the time is provisionally counted to (R17).

The diagonal stripes still mean unresolved. What changed is that you can now see, at a glance and without tapping, that two things were genuinely running.

Decision 03 · revised

Device stripes are labelled and tappable

A 10 pt anonymous stripe was the weakest part of v1 — you could see that a device was awake but not which one, and could not tap it. Columns are now 22 pt with a sticky rotated header carrying the device's short name, and every stripe is tappable with an expanded hit area.

They still do not get equal columns at phone width: R11 wants them present, not co-equal. On tablet and desktop they widen into full labelled columns automatically.

Decision 04

Editing belongs to the device event, never the combined block

The combined track is derived — it is computed from device events and stored nowhere. There is no "combined event" to open in the original app's editor.

So the combined detail lists its source slices and each one gets its own Edit button, opening the same editor the original app uses — bucket, id, start, end, and the editable data keys. Edit the truth, and the combined view recomputes.

Rejected

The chronological card list

Sessions in order, no time scale. Reads beautifully on a phone and is a fraction of the work.

It kills the feature. Contention shading is a claim about area. Drop proportional time and R8 has nothing left to say. Proportionality is non-negotiable here.

Honest cost

vis-timeline does not fully go away

vis-timeline is imported in seven files. Replacing all of it is a bigger project than this one, so two renderers coexist for a while — ours for the combined view and for Timeline at narrow width, vis-timeline for everything else.

That sits behind one component boundary rather than in a duplicated page, and it shrinks over time instead of growing.

Example data, not your real day. Two devices, hand-built to the shape of the 2026-09-09 verified run so the proportions and the contention are realistic. Totals, durations and overlaps are computed live — the combined total is a true union measure, never the sum of the devices (R6). Renames and theme persist in this browser only.
